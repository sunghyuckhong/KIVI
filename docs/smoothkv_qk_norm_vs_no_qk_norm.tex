\section{Setup and notation}

For a given decoder layer, denote the per-head dimension by $D$ (e.g., $D=128$ for Qwen3),
the number of query heads by $H_Q$, and the number of key/value heads by $H_{KV}$ (so
$H_Q=H_{KV}$ for plain MHA, and $H_Q > H_{KV}$ for GQA, with replication factor
$r = H_Q/H_{KV}$).

The standard attention block on a single query position computes
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Attn}(\Q, \K, \V)
\;=\; \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{\Q \K^\top}{\sqrt{D}}\right)\V .
\label{eq:attn}
\end{equation}

\textbf{SmoothKV}~\cite{smoothkv} migrates per-channel magnitude imbalance off
$\K$ (and optionally $\V$) so that downstream per-token int4 quantization sees
flatter K/V tensors. With per-channel scales $\sk \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$ and
$\sv \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$ (broadcasting per head), the fake-quant model is
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\K} \;=\; \big(\K / \sk\big) \cdot \sk, \qquad
\tilde{\V} \;=\; \big(\V / \sv\big) \cdot \sv,
\label{eq:smoothkv-fakequant}
\end{equation}
where the $/\sk$ step happens \emph{before} int4 quant, and the $\cdot \sk$
step happens \emph{after} dequant. Equation~(\ref{eq:smoothkv-fakequant})
is exactly equivalent to the un-smoothed identity at full precision; the
quantization error is absorbed across rebalanced channels.

For zero per-step runtime cost, we want to fold the $/\sk$ and $\cdot\sk$
factors into the surrounding model weights at load time. Where exactly we
fold depends on whether the architecture inserts an RMSNorm between
\texttt{qkv\_proj} and RoPE.

\section{Two architectural families}

\subsection*{Family A: no \texttt{qk\_norm}}

This is the older / vanilla pattern (Llama-2/3, Mistral, DSR1-Distill,
Qwen2). The forward path is:
\begin{equation}
h \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{qkv\_proj}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \text{reshape}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{RoPE}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \text{cache } \K
\label{eq:path-A}
\end{equation}
RoPE applies a per-channel rotation to a pair of channels; importantly,
no normalization sits between \texttt{qkv\_proj} and RoPE.

\subsection*{Family B: with \texttt{qk\_norm}}

This is the newer pattern adopted by Qwen3 / Qwen3-MoE / Olmo2. Each head's
$\Q$ and $\K$ pass through an RMSNorm \emph{before} RoPE:
\begin{equation}
h \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{qkv\_proj}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \text{reshape}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{q\_norm} / \mathrm{k\_norm}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{RoPE}
   \;\longrightarrow\; \text{cache } \K
\label{eq:path-B}
\end{equation}
The norm weights $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_q, \boldsymbol{\gamma}_k \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$
are \emph{shared across all heads}---each per-head $\Q$ slice gets the same
$\boldsymbol{\gamma}_q$ multiplied elementwise after the RMS normalization.
This shape constraint will dictate the entire fusion strategy below.

\section{Constraint 1: RoPE pair-equality}

RoPE applies a $2\!\times\!2$ rotation to channel pairs. Two conventions
appear in the wild:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Adjacent (GPTNeoX / KIVI-original Llama-2):} the pair is
    $(c, c+1)$, $c$ even; the rotation acts on $(K_c, K_{c+1})$ as a 2D vector.
  \item \textbf{rotate\_half (Llama-3 / Mistral / Qwen2 / Qwen3 / DSR1):} the
    pair is $(c, c+D/2)$; the rotation acts on $(K_c, K_{c+D/2})$.
\end{itemize}

For elementwise pre-RoPE scaling by $\sk$ to commute with RoPE---which we
\emph{need} because Eq.~(\ref{eq:smoothkv-fakequant}) divides by $\sk$
\emph{after} RoPE---we must have
\begin{equation}
\sk[c] = \sk[c'] \quad \text{for every RoPE-paired }(c,c') .
\label{eq:pair-equal}
\end{equation}
That is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item adjacent-RoPE models: enforce $\sk[2i] = \sk[2i+1]$. \texttt{make\_alpha\_variants.py --pair\_max\_k}.
  \item rotate\_half-RoPE models: enforce $\sk[i] = \sk[i+D/2]$. \texttt{make\_alpha\_variants.py --half\_pair\_max\_k}.
\end{itemize}

Both reduce the effective resolution of $\sk$ to $D/2$ \emph{distinct}
values per head per layer; the topology is just laid out differently.
Modern models (Qwen3 family) use rotate\_half, so we use \textbf{halfpair}.

\section{Constraint 2: where to fold $\sk$ and $\sv$}

\subsection{Family A (no \texttt{qk\_norm}): fold into \texttt{qkv\_proj} rows}

\texttt{qkv\_proj} is a single matrix whose output is split as
$[\Q\text{-block}, \K\text{-block}, \V\text{-block}]$ along the
output dim. Since \emph{no} normalization sits between \texttt{qkv\_proj}
and RoPE, any per-channel multiplier on a row of \texttt{qkv\_proj}
survives directly to post-RoPE $\K$.

We absorb (using row-wise broadcasting):
\begin{align}
W_{\mathrm{qkv}}[\text{rows of } \K] &\mathrel{/{=}} \sk \nonumber \\
W_{\mathrm{qkv}}[\text{rows of } \Q] &\mathrel{*{=}} \sk^{(r)}
   && \text{(broadcast over GQA replication factor $r$)} \nonumber \\
W_{\mathrm{qkv}}[\text{rows of } \V] &\mathrel{/{=}} \sv \nonumber \\
W_{\mathrm{o}}[\text{cols}]          &\mathrel{*{=}} \sv^{(r)}
\label{eq:fold-A}
\end{align}
Crucially, $\sk$ here may be \emph{per-($H_{KV}$, $D$)}---each $\K$ head
has its own $D$-dim row, applied to its own slice of $W_{\mathrm{qkv}}$.
After the rotate\_half / adjacent constraint of Eq.~(\ref{eq:pair-equal}),
the effective resolution is
\begin{equation}
\#\{\text{distinct } \sk \text{ values per layer (Family A)}\}
\;=\; H_{KV} \cdot D/2 .
\label{eq:resolution-A}
\end{equation}
For Qwen3-8B with $H_{KV}=8, D=128$: \textbf{$8 \cdot 64 = 512$} distinct
$\sk$ values per layer.

\subsection{Family B (with \texttt{qk\_norm}): fold into the gamma vectors}

Now there is an RMSNorm between \texttt{qkv\_proj} and RoPE:
\begin{equation}
\K \;=\; \mathrm{RMSNorm}\!\big(\K_{\mathrm{proj}}\big) \odot \boldsymbol{\gamma}_k,
\qquad \K_{\mathrm{proj}} \;=\; W_{\mathrm{qkv}}^{\K} h ,
\end{equation}
where $\odot$ denotes per-channel multiplication and $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_k
\in \mathbb{R}^{D}$. \textbf{Any per-channel scaling on the rows of}
$W_{\mathrm{qkv}}^{\K}$ \textbf{is destroyed by the RMS statistic}, because
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{RMSNorm}(\mathbf{s} \odot \mathbf{x}) \;\neq\; \mathbf{s} \odot \mathrm{RMSNorm}(\mathbf{x})
\quad\text{when } \mathbf{s} \text{ varies along the norm axis.}
\label{eq:rms-no-commute}
\end{equation}
The RMS denominator is itself a function of the rescaled vector, so the
per-channel scaling does not pass through.

Therefore we must fold \emph{post-norm}, into $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_k$ and
$\boldsymbol{\gamma}_q$ themselves:
\begin{equation}
\boldsymbol{\gamma}_q \mathrel{*{=}} \sk, \qquad \boldsymbol{\gamma}_k \mathrel{/{=}} \sk .
\label{eq:fold-B}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Head-uniform constraint.} Both $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_q$ and
$\boldsymbol{\gamma}_k$ have shape $(D,)$---\emph{they are shared across
every head}. There is no per-head dimension to fold a per-head $\sk$ into.
So the fused calib must satisfy
\begin{equation}
\sk^{(h_1)}[c] \;=\; \sk^{(h_2)}[c] \quad \forall h_1, h_2 \in \{1,\ldots,H_{KV}\},\ c \in \{1,\ldots,D\} .
\label{eq:head-uniform}
\end{equation}
This is what the \texttt{--head\_uniform\_k} flag enforces: it reduces
$\sk$ across the $H_{KV}$ axis (e.g., by max), then broadcasts the
result back so the runtime tensor shape is unchanged but every head
holds the same row.

After head-uniform reduction \emph{and} the rotate\_half pair-equality
constraint, the effective resolution becomes
\begin{equation}
\#\{\text{distinct } \sk \text{ values per layer (Family B)}\}
\;=\; D/2 .
\label{eq:resolution-B}
\end{equation}
For Qwen3-8B: \textbf{$64$} distinct $\sk$ values per layer---an
\textbf{$8\times$} reduction in expressivity vs.\ Family A on the same
architecture.

\paragraph{V-side fusion is unchanged.} $\V$ has neither a normalization
nor RoPE between \texttt{qkv\_proj} and the cache, so the V-side fold
into \texttt{qkv\_proj} V-rows and \texttt{o\_proj} input columns
(Eq.~\ref{eq:fold-A} last two lines) works identically for Family B
without any head-uniform constraint. Only the K-side suffers the
qk\_norm tax.

\section{Why the head-uniform tax is fundamental}

It is tempting to ask: can we keep per-head $\sk$ on a Family-B model and
fuse it somewhere else? The architectural shape of $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_k$
is the rate-limiter:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Pre-norm rows of $W_{\mathrm{qkv}}^{\K}$ have shape
    $(H_{KV} \cdot D)\times d_{\mathrm{model}}$. Folding here would let us
    encode per-head $\sk$, but Eq.~(\ref{eq:rms-no-commute}) makes that
    fusion non-equivalent to the runtime SmoothKV.
  \item $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_k$ has shape $(D)$. There is no per-head dim
    to absorb a per-head $\sk$ into.
  \item The post-RoPE / pre-cache stage offers no other learnable weight
    to fold into.
\end{itemize}
For zero-runtime cost on a qk\_norm model, the only deployable
$\sk$ is one shared across heads. This is not a limitation of the
SmoothKV calibration---it is a constraint of the architecture itself.

\section{Practical implications for KIVI / vLLM}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Calibration scripts (\texttt{run\_smoothkv\_calibrate.py},
    \texttt{make\_alpha\_variants.py}, \texttt{make\_percentile\_variants.py})
    auto-detect \texttt{qk\_norm} from the loaded model (presence of
    \texttt{q\_norm} / \texttt{k\_norm} sub-modules) and apply
    \texttt{--head\_uniform\_k} by default for Family B.
  \item The deployed fusion lives in
    \texttt{kivi\_vllm\_plugin/smoothkv\_fusion.py:fuse\_smoothkv\_into\_model}.
    It branches on \texttt{hasattr(module, "q\_norm") and hasattr(module,
    "k\_norm")} to choose Path A vs.\ Path B.
  \item Filename conventions:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Family A: \texttt{smoothkv\_<model>\_..\_a\{$\alpha$\}b\{$\beta$\}\_halfpair.pt}
      \item Family B: \texttt{smoothkv\_<model>\_..\_a\{$\alpha$\}b\{$\beta$\}\_huk\_halfpair.pt}
    \end{itemize}
  \item For evaluation on Qwen3-8B, the deployable headline number on
    minerva\_math500 (\texttt{math\_verify} metric, MG=32k retry) is
    $0.9476$ for $\alpha\!=\!\beta\!=\!1$ puremax + halfpair + HUK,
    matching or slightly beating BF16 ceiling at $0.9407$. The
    accompanying gsm8k cost is $77.86\%$ strict vs.\ BF16's $86.81\%$,
    a tax that the Family-A topology would not impose.
\end{enumerate}
